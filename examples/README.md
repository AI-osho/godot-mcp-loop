# Examples

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`platformer/`](platformer/) | The small platformer used by the quick start and the end-to-end tests. |
| [`blocks/`](blocks/), [`shooter/`](shooter/), [`action/`](action/), [`rpg/`](rpg/), [`marble/`](marble/) | Advanced samples: complete games generated by an AI agent through godot-mcp-loop, each from a single prompt, then verified independently. `marble/` is the 3D one. |
| [`fixtures/`](fixtures/) | Scenes used by the tests (e.g. one that raises a script error on purpose). |

## Advanced samples

Each game draws its pixel art from PNGs written with `image_write`, exposes test hooks (values for `game_set`, methods
for `game_call`) and ships regression scenarios in `scenarios/`.

### Falling Blocks — `blocks/blocks.tscn`

Generated by Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code). A falling-block puzzle: 10×20 board, 7 tetrominoes with SRS rotation and wall kicks, ghost piece,
NEXT preview, gravity that speeds up per level, 100/300/500/800 × level scoring.

- Controls: ←/→ move (auto-repeat), ↑ or X rotate right, Z rotate left, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, R restart
  (Enter after game over).
- Test hooks: `sequence` (fixed piece order such as `"OOT"`), `rng_seed`, `gravity_enabled`, `lines_per_level`; state
  such as `stack_text`, `piece_cells`, `spawn_history`. The `test_*.tscn` scenes preset these for the scenarios.

### Vertical shooter — `shooter/shooter.tscn`

Generated by Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code). 256×240 at integer scale; 4 enemy types across 5 waves, a 48×32 boss with
three attack patterns that speeds up below half health, explosions, a three-layer starfield, HUD with score, high
score, wave and lives.

- Controls: arrows or WASD move, Z / Space / J fire (hold for auto-fire), Enter start.
- Test hooks: `rng_seed`, `invincible`, `start_wave`, `waves_enabled`, `autopilot`, `fire_interval`, `max_shots`;
  `start_game(wave)`, `start_boss()`, `spawn_enemy(kind, x, y)`, `spawn_enemy_bullet(x, y, vx, vy)`,
  `damage_boss(n)`, `clear_enemies()`, `summary()`.

### Side-scroller — `action/action_game.tscn`

Generated by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (through Claude Code). 256×240 at integer scale; acceleration, variable jump height,
an 8-screen stage with pits, fixed and moving platforms, walking and hopping enemies (stomp them, touching from the
side costs a life), 42 coins, a goal flag with a time bonus, three-layer parallax, HUD with score, coins, lives and
time. All logic runs in `_physics_process` at 60 Hz.

- Controls: arrows or A/D move, Z / Space jump (hold for higher), Enter restart.
- Test hooks: `start_position`, `invincible`, `enemies_frozen`, `timer_frozen`; `start_game()`, `restart()`,
  `start_at(x, y)`, `set_player_position(x, y)`, `spawn_enemy(kind, x)`, `clear_enemies()`, `freeze_enemies(on)`,
  `stop_timer(on)`, `summary()`.

### RPG "星剣のルカ" (Luka of the Star Sword) — `rpg/main.tscn`

Generated by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (through Claude Code), including the story, characters and monsters; text is
Japanese. A young swordsman, Luka, is sent by King Orden to win back the Star Orb from the Dark King Zorga. Title
screen with a pixel-art logo, scrolling prologue, world map / town / castle with 16×16 grid movement, NPC dialogue
with gifts, random encounters on grass and in forests, front-view battles (attack, spells, herbs, flee), experience
and level-ups, defeat handling (back to the king with half the gold), a boss with a special attack, and an ending
with credits. Seven scripts (state machine, battle, data, rendering) and six scenarios. The text uses the system
Japanese font (Hiragino on macOS), so it needs one installed.

- Controls: arrows move (hold to keep walking), Z / Enter / Space confirm, X / Esc cancel.
- Test hooks: `rng_seed`, `encounters_enabled`, `encounter_rate`; `start_battle(key)`, `start_boss()`,
  `battle_command(cmd)`, `set_player_stats(stats)`, `warp(map, x, y)`, `face(dir)`, `start_move(dir)`,
  `show_message_instant(text)`, `finish_battle()`, `summary()`.
- After review, four small fixes were made outside the generating session (Hermes Agent, Claude Opus 5.5): holding
  a direction keeps walking, and three cosmetic overlaps (opening text, location banner, credits roll).

The shooter, side-scroller and RPG use input actions prefixed `shooter_`, `action_` and `rpg_` (defined in
`project.godot`).

### Marble Run — `marble/main.tscn` (3D)

Generated by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (through Claude Code) from a BRIEF that asked for a 3D marble game: a rolling ball down a
ramp, over 3 m and 2 m gaps that need a jump, across a 1.8 m beam, 10 coins, two checkpoints, three lives, a chase camera
and a timed clear. This is the sample the 3D tools were hardened against (`game_in_view`, `game_ray`, `game_contacts`,
`record.method`, JSON `Transform3D` values).

- Controls: arrows or WASD roll the marble away from the camera, Space jumps (grounded only), Enter starts and returns
  to the title.
- Test hooks: `start_game()`, `teleport_ball(x, y, z)`, `set_checkpoint(index)`,
  `summary()` → `{state, coins, lives, time_frames, ball_pos, ball_speed, on_floor, checkpoint}`.
- The course is a staircase: each landing is 0.6 m higher than the one before, so a gap cannot be crossed by rolling off
  the edge — `7_gap_needs_jump.yaml` proves both halves (forward alone falls, a jump at speed lands on the far platform).

This copy is generated from [`samples/marble-3d`](../samples/marble-3d/) (the standalone project that ships with
`npx godot-mcp-loop sample marble-3d`); `node scripts/sync-marble-example.mjs` rewrites its `res://` paths, and a test
fails when the two drift apart. The design reasons live in [`samples/marble-3d/DESIGN.md`](../samples/marble-3d/DESIGN.md).
